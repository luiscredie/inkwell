────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BOTH THINGS YOU SPOTTED HAVE ONE CAUSE

I sized the display numerals for the slot and not for the string. Brazilian
currency runs long, and it broke in two places:

FALTANDO (Now Playing hero)
  missingText concatenated two different units into one string — "4 · R$ 179,60" —
  at 26px bold. It wrapped mid-value and left an orphan "4 ·" on the first line.

VALOR DA COLEÇÃO (Overview KPI)
  "R$ 11.732,60" at a fixed 44px inside a tile whose column floor was 210px. It
  clipped at the "6".

In both cases the number is the most important thing on the tile, and the number
was the part you could not read.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE FIX

fitSize(str, max, mid, min) picks the size from the STRING LENGTH, not the slot:
6 characters or fewer get the full size, up to 9 the middle, longer than that the
smallest. Run against real values:

  4 · 67% · 2.047              44px
  R$ 179,60                    34px
  R$ 11.732,60                 27px
  R$ 1.234.567,89              27px

Applied to the four Overview KPIs and the three Collection summary tiles, with
overflow-wrap:anywhere as a backstop and the grid column floor raised 210 -> 248px.

The Faltando tile now has three lines of its own: count at 26px, cost in gold mono
at 12.5px, then the label. Two units, two lines.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
