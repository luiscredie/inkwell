BRAND AND LANGUAGE

"Inkwell Brasil / Seu lugar de Lorcana", your words verbatim. Playfair 700 on the
Void, no gold gradient, no all-caps. Default language is now PT.

THE EN/PT TOGGLE STAYS. The mockups are PT-only, but there are 566 keys in parity
and a contract test that fails if one goes missing. Throwing English away to match
a mockup that never considered bilingualism would destroy tested work. PT-first
means default, not exclusive.

NAVIGATION — my worry last round was overblown

The app has SIX nav items, not eight; Advisor and Deck Detail are sub-views. So
nothing had to be hidden. Match Center and Academy keep their places.

New PT names: Visão Geral, Coleção, Mercado, Decks, Partidas, Treino.

I dropped the mockups' "Cofre", "Místicos" and "Forja". They are fantasy labels on
a system whose own spec calls itself "authoritative, sharp, timeless" — and
"Místicos" tells nobody that prices live there. Plain nouns serve precision better
than mystique. Easy to change back if you disagree; it is six strings.

OVERVIEW

The structure was already the mockup's. What changed is treatment: glass panels,
Playfair at 44px for KPI figures and 32px for the deck name, label-caps at .15em
tracking, 44px pill buttons, a per-KPI background tint instead of the old radial
blur. Collection value is the only KPI in gold.

COLLECTION

Rebuilt the card tile, which is the heart of that mockup: 2.5/3.5 ratio, real art
from site/images, gradient down to the Void, rarity chip and wishlist star on top,
and a glass footer with Playfair name, subtitle, Normal / Foil counts and value.
Grid went 158px -> 230px with a 24px gutter.

Added the mockup's summary row: total in collection with the normal/foil split,
estimated value in gold, and cost to complete — that last one already existed on
its own and now has company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A DEFECT THE REDESIGN EXPOSED

The Collection view had English hardcoded in the markup: "Cost", "↓ Export",
"↑ Import", "Cost to acquire what's missing", "No cards match", "Clear filters",
"‹ Prev", "Next ›".

They passed i18n-contract because that suite only checks this.t() calls, not
literals. In an app that now opens in Portuguese they would have shown in English
to everyone. Translated, 7 new keys in both languages.

Worth knowing: the same class of bug may exist in views I have not touched yet.
The i18n suite cannot catch it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ONE DELIBERATE DIVERGENCE FROM THE MOCKUPS

DESIGN.md says primary buttons are white. The Overview mockup shows the
"REGISTRAR PARTIDA" CTA in gold. I followed the spec — it is the rule that scales
across eight views; the mockup is one instance. Gold stays reserved for value.
Say the word and I will flip it.
