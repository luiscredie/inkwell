TYPOGRAPHY — Inter
The guide names Inter explicitly, and that is now the second guide asking for it.
Switched from Plus Jakarta Sans.

Why this does not contradict you: you pointed at "Atividade de Partidas" and said
you liked that font, in a message whose actual point was "no Garamond, no serif".
Inter and Plus Jakarta are both humanist grotesques and are near-indistinguishable
at that label size. The real request — no serif — still holds. One line to revert
if you disagree.

Weight scale applied where it does something: 300 on each page's lead paragraph,
500 on uppercase labels (they were 700).

One deviation: the guide says 300 for body. I did not apply that globally — at
12-13px on #0a0c10, Inter Light goes thin and loses contrast. 300 is used only
where the text is big enough to carry it.

WHY I DID NOT BUILD SECTIONS A, B AND C

I audited the data before writing any of them. Most of what those three screens
display does not exist in this app:

  A · Ink Log Analyst
      inkability, curve, mulligan .............. HAVE
      per-card play rate / win-when-played ..... DO NOT — match logs are not
                                                 recorded per card
      goldfish simulator "T-5.4" ............... DO NOT — no turn simulator exists

  B · Deck Strategy & Wishlist
      arbitrage BRL vs USD ..................... DO NOT — only LigaLorcana, in BRL
      portfolio ROI ............................ DO NOT — the collection stores
                                                 quantities, not what you paid
      meta decay / obsolescence risk ........... DO NOT — meta-decks.json is
                                                 popularity, no tournament data

  C · Intelligence Hub
      volatility alerts + sparklines ........... HAVE (price history, movers)
      meta-game share % ........................ DO NOT
      advisor insights ......................... PARTIAL (AI Coach exists)

Building those panels now would mean inventing numbers. "42% obsolescence risk",
"T-5.4 average turn to win", "-12% spread vs global" are placeholders in a mockup;
in the shipped app they would be lies wearing the costume of data. This is the same
trap M2.4 avoided when it refused tier language without tournament results.

WHAT I CAN BUILD NOW WITH NO INVENTION

  1. Inkability panel (A) — inkable vs non-inkable percentage for a deck. This is
     literally what the guide's own "Logic" line asks for, and the data is there.
  2. Volatility alerts with sparklines (C) — from the price history already
     feeding the movers list.
  3. Wishlist with target price (B) — the wishlist exists; it needs a target field
     and a marker for when the market crosses it.

Everything else needs new data first: acquisition cost captured at import, a USD
price feed, and tournament-result ingestion.

Tell me which of the three to build, or which data source to wire up first.
