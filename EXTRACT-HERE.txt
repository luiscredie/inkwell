1. ACQUISITION COST — the data that unlocks the rest

parseMoney() takes both formats that occur in practice: R$ 1.234,56 (pt-BR) and
1,234.56 (en). Junk, blank and negative all return null — never 0, because "paid
nothing" and "cost unknown" are different facts and collapsing them would wreck
every average built on top.

Columns read in BOTH CSV branches (Dreamborn and native):
  price · unit price · paid · purchase price · preço · custo · valor pago
  plus the total variants. Total wins over unit when a file has both.

DATA SHAPE: acquisition[cardId] = {paid: total, qty: copies}

Storing the total rather than a unit price is what keeps the average honest: buy 2
at 10, then 1 at 40, and the average is 20 (60/3) — not 25 ((10+40)/2). There is a
test for exactly that case.

Cost ALWAYS accumulates, whatever collection merge mode you pick. Importing the
same purchase twice will double-count — but replacing would silently throw away
what an earlier file told you, which is worse. So it accumulates and reports:
the audit now carries rows_with_cost and cards_costed. The pre-import rollback
snapshot includes cost too, otherwise undoing an import would orphan it.

2. PORTFOLIO ROI

Paid versus current value — over ONLY the copies whose cost is known. Mixing known
and unknown would produce a number that looks like a total and is not.

Three deliberate refusals:
  - no recorded cost at all means NO ROI. There is no "0%".
  - owning 10 copies with cost known for 2 compares 2. It does not extrapolate.
  - a card with recorded cost you no longer own is skipped, or a card you sold
    would inflate your total paid forever.

The panel states its coverage ("X of Y copies have a known cost") and says how many
were left out. If your file has no price column, the import preview says so and
tells you what to add.

3. M2.6 BEGINNER JOURNEY

The app already had five lesson tracks. What was missing was not more content — it
was ORDER. A library of lessons does not answer "so what now?".

Six steps, each answered by your real state rather than a checkbox: read the
lesson, recorded a card, created a deck, got a deck to 60, logged a match, reviewed
a replay. Nothing is hand-ticked.

Exactly one step is "current" at any moment, and a BLOCKED step never becomes the
current one: reviewing a replay needs a match WITH a log, and pointing you at
something impossible is worse than pointing at nothing. The block explains itself
instead of just going grey.

It is now the first Academy tab and the default, because it is the answer for
someone who opens the app not knowing what to do.
