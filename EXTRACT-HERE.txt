REPLAY — VIDEO-PLAYER MODEL

Researching how digital TCG clients present replays confirmed the choice you had
already made on the form: a draggable timeline with per-turn chapters beats a
next-event stepper. You cannot answer "what happened around turn 6" by clicking
forty times.

  - real <input type="range"> over the event index
  - turn chapters from replayChapters(), one per turn, contiguous ranges
  - the M2.5 decision moments as clickable markers on the bar
  - speed 0.5x / 1x / 2x

Dragging pauses playback, so the scrub and the timer never fight. Changing speed
mid-playback restarts the timer at the new rate instead of waiting out the old tick.

THREE REAL BUGS THE TESTS FOUND

1. "Your turn begins" WAS NOT RECOGNISED
   The turn pattern accepted "Player 1", "You", "You's" and "Opponent" — but not
   "Your", which is the form a real log actually uses. Such a replay produced NO
   chapters at all, leaving the timeline undivided. Fixed in all three places that
   share the pattern (chapters, moments, simulation), and player attribution now
   accepts any form starting with "You".

2. THE INKABILITY PANEL WAS INVISIBLE ON A HEALTHY DECK
   It sat INSIDE the deck-warnings sc-if. No warnings meant no panel — precisely
   the deck where you want to confirm the ratio is fine. The health card now has
   its own condition; warning chips stay conditional, and a clean deck gets a
   positive line instead of an empty card.

3. OPPONENT INK: COMPUTED, NEVER RENDERED
   oppInkPips existed in renderVals and appeared nowhere in the template. Worse,
   the opponent row showed exerted/total while your row showed available/total —
   the same glyph read in opposite directions on the two sides of the board. Both
   rows are now identical: gold pips plus available-over-total.

   The pips also carried a boolean where the template wanted a colour, so they were
   painting background:true.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
INKABILITY

Inkable percentage with a bar, inkable/non-inkable counts, and one descriptive
sentence per band (>=55% fine, >=45% borderline, below that missed ink drops are
likely). Reads inkableOf(), which already honours your corrections.

WISHLIST

Per-card target price with a hit marker when the market crosses it. Accepts a
decimal comma, rounds to cents, and refuses junk input rather than storing NaN.
Legacy entries (plain 1, no target) keep working — no wishlist is lost.
