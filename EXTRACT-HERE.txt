1. THE LIVING INK BACKGROUND — MY BUG

The shader was fine and running. #ink-scroll still carried a two-radial background
left over from V7. The canvas is position:fixed at z-index:0 and #ink-scroll sits
above it, so the content layer painted over the entire shader. I had cleared the
background on #app but not on the scroller inside it.

  #ink-scroll{background:transparent}

One line. The lesson is worth keeping: moving to a global background means clearing
EVERY intermediate layer, not just the root — any wrapper with its own background
cancels everything beneath it.

2. COLLECTION — THE CARD TEXT WAS COVERED

The glass footer sat ON TOP of the image, hiding the bottom third — which is
exactly where a Lorcana card prints its name and rules text. There was also a dark
gradient over the art purely so white text would have contrast.

Inverted. The art is now shown whole and unobstructed; name, counts and price moved
BELOW the image. The gradient is gone — it existed to solve a problem that no
longer exists.

Counts are now compact mono ("4  2 foil") instead of three labelled blocks. At 5-8
columns there is no width for labels, and the glass footer forced a fixed height
that cropped the card.

3. MIXED LANGUAGES

Swept the whole file. 41 English strings were showing while the app was in
Portuguese:

  25 template literals   Cancel, No grouping, Sign out, Backup & Settings,
                         Pipeline report, Reload, Win, Loss, ...
  12 toasts              showToast('Backup downloaded'), 'Deck deleted',
                         'No cards matched — check the format', ... — none of
                         these ever went through this.t()
   3 attributes          aria-label="Close" (×3), two title attributes
   1 mixed sentence      "Signed in as <b>email</b>. Your profiles sync..."

All now keyed in EN and PT. The dictionary went from 584 to 617 keys per language.

Worth noting how they survived: i18n-contract checks parity and that used keys
exist, but it cannot see text that was NEVER WIRED to the dictionary. That is the
gap 41 strings lived in. The scan for literal text nodes and raw showToast strings
is now part of how I check this file.

4. MOBILE OVERLAP

The performance pill was fixed bottom-right, directly on top of the bottom nav bar.
Hidden below 860px — and on those screens the shader is force-disabled anyway, so
the control had nothing to control.
