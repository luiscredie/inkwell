ALL FIVE PIECES

1. DECISION MARKERS (Match Center)
   matchMoments() segments the replay by turn and compares game state at the
   start and end of each turn, using M2.0's own simulateReplay rather than a
   second parser — so the markers can never disagree with the board you see.

   Three labelled axes per turn:
     Board  — change in characters and effective STR/WILL on both sides
     Lore   — lore gained this turn, plus detection of the lead changing hands
     Ink    — total cost played by each side

   A turn only becomes a moment when it crosses a threshold (4 board swing or a
   2-character difference; 3 lore or a lead flip; 4 ink difference). Quiet turns
   stay quiet.

2. "3 MOMENTS TO REVIEW"
   The three highest combined magnitude, re-sorted into turn order so the list
   reads like the game happened rather than like a leaderboard. Each jumps the
   replay to that exact turn.

3. DESCRIPTIVE ONLY — as you asked
   "Characters on the field — you -2, opponent 0." Never "you should have".
   A standing note explains why: replay logs carry no hidden information, so
   nothing here can judge what you were holding. There is a test that scans the
   string block for prescriptive vocabulary in both languages.

4. MATCHUP NOTEBOOK (Academy)
   Keyed by opponent ink colours with the pairing sorted, so Amber/Steel and
   Steel/Amber are one matchup, not two. Shows your record and win rate beside
   the note field.

   A matchup you have written a note about but never played still appears —
   dropping someone's writing because they have no games yet would be data loss.
   With no games the win rate reads "—", never 0%.

5. MULLIGAN LAB (Academy)
   Reuses M1.9's mulliganHand and adds mulliganScore: plays by 3 ink, inkable
   count, 6+ cost cards, average cost.

   The score appears only AFTER you commit to keep or mulligan. Showing it first
   would just be giving you the answer. The verdict is compared against your call
   and the basis is on screen, with the caveat that it knows nothing about your
   matchup or your plan.

   A hand with early plays but nothing inkable is still a mulligan — tested.

6. WEEKLY GOALS (Academy)
   Log 3 matches, review 2 replays, practise 5 opening hands. The week is keyed
   to its Monday, so progress resets on its own — no cron, no expiry timestamp,
   no server. Last week's progress is never counted toward this week.

   Logging already counted itself; reviewing counts when you jump to a moment;
   practising counts when you draw a hand.

PERSISTENCE
   matchupNotes and goalProgress were added to userPayload, to migrateUser with
   defaults, and to BOTH load paths (local and cloud pull). Older payloads
   without these fields load normally.
