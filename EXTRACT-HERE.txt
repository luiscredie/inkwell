WHAT I DID WITH THE ART

Your 1024x1024 / 2.1 MB render became a 256x256 PNG: the flat outer border of the
render is cropped away (3.5%) and a circular mask applied, so the coin sits on any
surface without a square frame around it. 2.1 MB in a sidebar icon would have been
the single heaviest asset on the page.

Placed in three spots:
  - rail brand mark, 44px, beside the wordmark
  - onboarding, 132px, with a shadow instead of the old gold glow (the coin is
    already gold; the glow doubled up)
  - FAVICON — the app had none at all

No ink/logo.svg references remain.

At 40-44px the engraved text is not legible. That is correct for a seal-style
mark: the coin reads as gold and the wordmark beside it carries the name. I did
not try to make the text readable at that size — it would need a simplified mark,
which is a different piece of art.
